Bridal creeper

Asparagus asparagoides
High-priority invasive weed in Western Australia

Bridal creeper is a climbing perennial with glossy, heart-shaped leaves and small white flowers. It forms dense underground mats that smother native plants and is most visible through winter and spring.

Look for

White star flowers Glossy leaves Red berries

Site Description

Bold Park is a significant bushland reserve in Perth, Western Australia, protecting Banksia woodland, coastal heath and native plant communities across the Swan Coastal Plain.
